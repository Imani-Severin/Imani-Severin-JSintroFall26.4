About

Hi, I’m Imani Severin-Jarosz—a fullstack web developer, classical musician, and audio enthusiast dedicated to helping fellow artists, musicians, and arts organizations elevate their digital presence. I build clean, custom, and responsive websites tailored specifically for the creative community.

My work in web development is shaped by my roots in the arts. I am a classically trained soprano and proficient pianist with a Master of Music in Vocal Performance from the Cleveland Institute of Music and a Bachelor of Music from the University of West Florida. Having spent years working as a private voice and piano instructor, I intimately understand the unique needs, branding, and workflows of independent musicians and creative spaces.

Because I love bridging art and technology, I am currently expanding my technical expertise through the Code the Dream full-stack software development intensive, building strong foundations in JavaScript, HTML, CSS, and modern web workflows.

Beyond web development, I am actively self-teaching interactive audio implementation and game audio tools like FMOD Studio and REAPER DAW, with the ultimate goal of stepping into the industry as a Technical Sound Designer. Whether I'm coding a custom portfolio site for an ensemble or exploring dynamic audio systems, I enjoy combining my musicianship with the problem-solving mindset of a developer.
